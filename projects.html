I've built many projects and programs over the years, from study tools for students to apps for real businesses. These are a few of my most recent ones.

Study tool

Bench

A CBT practice app for students. You upload a question bank as a PDF, text file or CSV, and the app pulls out every question, option and answer. Then you can sit timed practice tests just like the real exam.

  • Exam mode or practice mode, with a timer and pass mark
  • Every attempt is scored and saved so you can see your weak areas
  • Works offline and keeps your data in your own browser
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • AI parsing

Student platform

Matric

An all-in-one app for Nigerian university students. It handles the small things students pay for and keep track of every week, all in one dashboard.

  • Wallet for airtime, data, electricity and exam PINs
  • GPA / CGPA calculator and deadline alerts
  • Backend with sign-in, a wallet and transaction history
  • HTML/CSS/JS
